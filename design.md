# EthAum — UI/UX Design System

## 1. Design Goal

EthAum should feel like a real premium healthcare company, not an AI-generated SaaS template.

Design references:
- Premium healthcare
- International travel
- Modern product UX
- Calm editorial websites

## 2. Visual Personality

- Calm
- Human
- Trustworthy
- Premium
- International
- Spacious
- Professional
- Accessible

Avoid:
- Neon colors
- Purple AI gradients
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Futuristic medical graphics
- Excessive rounded cards
- Huge decorative animations
- Fake-looking medical imagery

## 3. Color Direction

Use neutral healthcare tones:

```text
Warm White      #F8F8F5
Soft Grey       #EEF1EF
Soft Sage       #E6EFEB
Muted Green     #315B55
Deep Green      #173E39
Charcoal        #172321
Warm Beige      #F1EEE8
```

Use accent colors primarily for:
- Primary actions
- Focus states
- Selection
- Status
- Important interaction

## 4. Typography

Recommended:
- Inter
- Geist
- Manrope

Optional editorial heading:
- DM Serif Display
- Libre Baskerville

Use serif typography sparingly for major storytelling headlines.

## 5. Interaction Philosophy

The uploaded video reference should guide interaction style rather than be copied visually.

Use:
- Smooth section reveals
- Subtle image parallax
- Hover elevation
- Scroll-based journey storytelling
- Gentle page transitions
- Interactive cards
- Progressive disclosure
- Sticky navigation
- Smooth horizontal treatment carousels

Avoid:
- Constant motion
- Excessive 3D
- Glowing AI effects
- Animation on every element

## 7. Hero

Headline:

> World-Class Healthcare Beyond Boundaries

Supporting copy:

> Compare hospitals, explore treatment options, and manage your complete healthcare journey — all in one place.

Primary CTA:
`Find Treatment`

Secondary CTA:
`Create Medical Case`

Search:
- Treatment/procedure
- Destination
- Search

Use realistic photography.

## 8. Signature Interaction

The Medical Case should be the visual identity of the product.

Example:

```text
Medical Case
Knee Replacement
India

✓ Case Created

✓ AI Organization
● Provider Matching
○ Quotes

```

## 9. AI UX

AI should feel embedded into the workflow.

Use:

`Organizing your records...`

instead of:

`AI MAGIC ✨`

Use:

`Preparing your case summary...`

instead of:

`AI is thinking...`

## 10. Accessibility

- Strong contrast
- Keyboard navigation
- Visible focus states
- Accessible labels
- 44px+ touch targets
- Alt text
- Status should not depend only on color
- Responsive layouts

## 11. Responsive Strategy

Desktop:
- Editorial multi-column layouts
- Large photography
- Side-by-side content

Tablet:
- Reduce columns
- Preserve hierarchy

Mobile:
- Bottom navigation for logged-in patients
- Large touch targets
- Stacked forms
- Easy document upload
- Simplified provider comparison
