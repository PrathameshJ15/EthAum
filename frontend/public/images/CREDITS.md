# EthAum Image Credits & License Log

This file records all photographic assets used across EthAum in strict compliance with DESIGN.md Section 7.

## Standard Rules
- Real, documentary, natural light, calm clinical spaces.
- No AI-generated people.
- Photos of people are illustrative; never captioned as real EthAum patients or doctors.
- All photos have documented permissive open licenses (Unsplash Open License / CC0).
- No hotlinking; all assets are bundled locally in `frontend/public/images/`.
